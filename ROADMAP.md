# Mona's Bookmark Roadmap

A quick look at what's planned for Mona's Bookmark Manager and the risks to watch.

| Area | Item | Type | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Search & filter bookmarks | 🚀 | High |
| Bookmark management | Edit a bookmark | 🚀 | Medium |
| Organization | Tags or folders | 🚀 | Medium |
| Portability | Import & export bookmarks | 🚀 | Medium |
| Slugs | Duplicate slugs on re-add | 🐞 | High |
| Validation | Empty-URL validation | 🐞 | Medium |
| Data safety | Losing bookmarks when localStorage is cleared | 🐞 | Medium |

- 📦 Import & export bookmarks — priority: medium

## 🐞 Known bugs & risks

- 🐞 Duplicate slugs on re-add — priority: high
- 🐞 Empty-URL validation — priority: medium
- 🐞 Losing bookmarks when localStorage is cleared — priority: medium
